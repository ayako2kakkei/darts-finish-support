Privacy Policy

Darts Finish · Effective date: 4 November 2026

Darts Finish (“the app”) does not collect personal information. This policy explains what the app handles and how.

Stored on your device

Your settings (such as the chosen rules and preferred double), any set-ups you have edited, and your “Learnt!” marks are stored on your device only. They are used solely so you can continue practising and are never sent off the device.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information. There are no accounts, adverts, analytics or tracking. Your device’s language setting is read on the device only to choose the display language.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device also deletes all of its saved settings and records.

Changes to this policy

If the way the app handles information changes, for example when new features are added, this page will be updated.
